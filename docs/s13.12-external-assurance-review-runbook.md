# S13.12 — External Assurance Review

## Purpose
Conduct the independent-review gate over the reconciled S13 event package and anchor candidate.

This gate determines whether the evidence and controls are supportable within the approved assurance scope. It does not create environmental observations.

## Procedure
1. Freeze the assurance scope, reporting period/event and population.
2. Confirm reviewer identity, independence/authority and access.
3. Assemble the controlled evidence-room package.
4. Review evidence lineage from source/field records through DMRV derivations.
5. Review methodology, organizational/operational boundaries and factor versions.
6. Review the S13.10 reconciliation conclusion and material exceptions.
7. Review the S13.11 anchor candidate against the locked package digest.
8. Classify findings and limitations.
9. Require documented CAPA/disposition for findings where applicable.
10. Record the assurance conclusion, scope, limitations and responsible authority.
11. Lock the review record.

## Outcomes
- **NO_MATERIAL_FINDINGS_WITHIN_SCOPE** — no material findings identified within approved scope.
- **FINDINGS_WITHIN_SCOPE** — findings are documented without implying failure outside scope.
- **CONDITIONAL_PENDING_CAPA** — conclusion depends on specified corrective action.
- **INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE** — evidence cannot support the reviewed scope.
- **HOLD** — material issue prevents conclusion.

## Evidence boundary
External assurance is a review conclusion about the defined evidence and scope. It does not create an environmental observation and does not automatically constitute certification, regulatory approval, premium-condition determination or product release.

An anchor candidate remains an integrity/reference artifact. Review does not turn an anchor into environmental truth.

## Fail-closed
No conclusion is authorized when scope, reviewer authority, material evidence, lineage, methodology/boundary/factor controls, reconciliation or anchor linkage is unresolved.

## Current declaration
No S13.12 external assurance review or conclusion is claimed. No reviewer finding or assurance statement is fabricated.
