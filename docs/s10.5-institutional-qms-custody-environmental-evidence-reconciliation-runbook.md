# S10.5 — Institutional QMS, Custody and Environmental Evidence Reconciliation

## Purpose
S10.5 connects the locked disclosure evidence population to environmental sampling, chain-of-custody and QMS/laboratory records. It reconciles provenance; it does not manufacture empirical results.

## Reconciliation sequence
EVIDENCE POPULATION LOCK → SITE/EVENT IDENTITY → SAMPLE IDENTITY → CUSTODY CHAIN → QMS METHOD/RESULT CHECK → BATCH/SEAL LINKAGE → DMRV LINEAGE CHECK → EXCEPTION REVIEW → INDEPENDENT REVIEW HANDOFF.

## QMS record
A controlled record carries sample_id, batch_id where applicable, collection_event_id, collector_id, custody transfers, laboratory_id, method/version, result references, result status, chain-of-custody status, quality flags and evidence references.

## State separation
QMS readiness, sample receipt, analytical review and validated result are separate states. Chain of custody establishes provenance of transfer; it does not by itself establish analytical validity.

## Evidence rules
- Sample identity remains stable across custody and QMS.
- Laboratory result references must identify the method/version and source record.
- A batch/seal reference cannot create a batch that is not supported by physical records.
- Missing evidence is not zero.
- Administrative reporting coverage is not physical harvest evidence.

## Fail closed
Hold reconciliation when sample identity or custody is broken, method/version is absent, result provenance is missing, batch/sample linkage conflicts, material quality flags are unresolved, DMRV references disagree, or a result is being promoted beyond its evidence state.

## Physical boundary
S10.5 is a reconciliation control. It does not create rainfall, collection, sample, laboratory, batch, premium or regulatory evidence.
