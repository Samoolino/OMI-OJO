# S9.4 — Independent Assurance and Governed Scale Certification Readiness

## Purpose
S9.4 creates a controlled readiness gate for independent assurance. It does **not** declare that assurance or certification has occurred.

## Independent assurance contract
A review package must identify:
- reviewer identity and authority;
- conflict-of-interest status;
- review period;
- population and sampling scope;
- methodology/version;
- evidence references;
- exception/CAPA references;
- limitations;
- conclusion state.

Owner or management attestation is not an independent assurance conclusion.

## Climate and ESG conformity
The readiness review tests whether each material metric can be traced through:
SOURCE → TRANSFORMATION → CALCULATION → RECONCILIATION → REVIEW → CLAIM/DISCLOSURE.

For GHG reporting, the package must preserve organizational boundary, operational boundary, Scope 1/2/3 classification, activity data, emission factors, calculation method, reporting period and change/restatement context where applicable.

For IFRS S2, the control surface maps governance, strategy, risk management and metrics/targets, while recognizing that materiality and the reporting entity's applicable requirements determine the actual disclosure.

For ESRS E1, use the current adopted requirements applicable to the reporting perimeter and jurisdiction. The 2026 revised ESRS Delegated Act is treated as the current adopted reference in this implementation; earlier EFRAG technical advice/draft material is not represented as adopted law.

## Evidence-state discipline
READINESS ≠ ASSURANCE ≠ CERTIFICATION ≠ PRODUCT RELEASE.

A reviewer may conclude only within the defined population, period, methodology and evidence scope.

## Fail-closed conditions
HOLD when reviewer authority/conflict information is absent, the population or period is undefined, evidence lineage is broken, material exceptions lack disposition, GHG boundary/methodology is incomplete, standards versions are unidentified, limitations are omitted, or a claim exceeds the reviewed evidence scope.

## Physical boundary
Physical/product status remains NOT_RELEASED. No assurance-readiness state establishes that rainwater was harvested, bottled, laboratory-tested, designated premium, or regulatorily released.
