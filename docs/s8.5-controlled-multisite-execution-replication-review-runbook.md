# S8.5 — Controlled Multisite Execution and Replication Review

## Objective
Run the approved replication controls at independently admitted sites and determine whether the controls remain conforming without transferring environmental evidence between sites.

## Execution sequence
**SCOPE LOCK → LOCAL AUTHORIZATION → READINESS → PRE-EVENT INSPECTION → RAIN WATCH → FIRST FLUSH → COLLECTION → CUSTODY/QMS → SITE DMRV → EVIDENCE RECONCILIATION → INDEPENDENT REVIEW → MULTISITE COMPARABILITY → CAPA → CLOSEOUT**

## Site isolation
Every site retains its own:
- site identity;
- authorization record;
- operator/equipment evidence;
- event and observation records;
- samples and custody chain;
- QMS evidence;
- DMRV package;
- evidence digest/anchor;
- deviations and CAPA.

No record from one site may be silently reused as evidence for another site.

## Cross-site review
Reviewers may compare:
- control completion;
- evidence completeness;
- provenance quality;
- conformance outcomes;
- exception patterns;
- measurement methods where equivalent and documented.

Reviewers must not treat administrative coverage, common methodology or shared software as proof that environmental values are equal or interchangeable.

## Closeout states
**SITE_CONFORMING** — controls and evidence are conforming for the reviewed scope.

**SITE_CONDITIONAL** — explicit deficiencies/conditions remain.

**SITE_HOLD / SITE_NONCONFORMING** — promotion stops until the deficiency is resolved.

**MULTISITE_REVIEW_COMPLETE_PENDING_NEXT_GATE** — all reviewed sites have documented outcomes and the portfolio is ready for the next gate.

None of these states establish premium status, regulatory approval, potable status or commercial product release.
