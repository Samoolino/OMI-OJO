# S13.7 — QMS / Laboratory Handoff

## Purpose
Control admission of the actual event sample into the approved QMS/laboratory pathway while preserving sample identity, custody, requested method and receipt provenance.

This gate does not create a laboratory result.

## Preconditions
- S13.6 custody has actually been reconciled.
- Sample identifier is unique and linked to the event.
- Approved QMS/laboratory identity is available.
- Receiving role/person can accept the sample.

## Procedure
1. Confirm the complete S13.6 custody record.
2. Verify the receiving QMS/laboratory identity.
3. Reconcile the sample identifier and container/integrity reference.
4. Record actual laboratory admission/receipt timestamp.
5. Record the requested test panel or method reference.
6. Record sample condition at receipt where required.
7. Obtain receiving acceptance or documented rejection.
8. Preserve the admission and acceptance record.
9. Place the sample/event on HOLD when material custody, integrity or admission evidence is unresolved.

## Evidence boundary
Laboratory admission is a QMS/process record, not a laboratory result. A requested test or method is not a result. Only an actual laboratory analysis may create a QMS laboratory result. QMS admission does not establish premium condition or product release.

## Fail-closed
Do not treat the sample as analyzed or validly tested when custody is unresolved, laboratory identity is missing, admission timestamp is absent, requested method is undefined, receipt condition is missing where required, or acceptance is absent.

## Current declaration
No S13.7 laboratory admission or laboratory result is claimed. No laboratory value is fabricated.
