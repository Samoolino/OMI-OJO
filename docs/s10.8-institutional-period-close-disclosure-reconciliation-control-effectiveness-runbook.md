# S10.8 — Institutional Period Close, Disclosure Reconciliation and Control Effectiveness

## Purpose
S10.8 closes a reporting period after reconciliation of evidence, metric lineage, methodologies, QMS/custody records, assurance dispositions, authorized claims and public/investor surfaces.

## Sequence
PERIOD SCOPE FREEZE → EVIDENCE POPULATION → METRIC LINEAGE → METHOD/FACTOR VERSION → QMS/CUSTODY/EXCEPTIONS → ASSURANCE/CLAIMS → PUBLIC/INVESTOR SURFACES → CONTROL EFFECTIVENESS → OPEN EXCEPTIONS/CAPA → CLOSE AUTHORIZATION → IMMUTABLE CLOSE RECORD → NEXT-PERIOD CARRY-FORWARD.

## Control effectiveness
Each material control is assessed as DESIGNED, OPERATING_EFFECTIVE, OPERATING_WITH_EXCEPTION, INEFFECTIVE or NOT_TESTED. The conclusion concerns the control and its tested evidence, not environmental truth.

## Period-close record
The close record retains period ID, scope/boundaries, evidence population, lineage digest, methodology/factor versions, QMS/custody references, assurance and claim references, surface reconciliation, open exceptions/CAPA, control tests, close authority/timestamp and successor-period reference.

## Restatement discipline
A closed period is not silently overwritten. Material correction uses a controlled successor/revision record with reason, affected claims/metrics, evidence references, authorization and linkage to the prior record.

## Fail closed
Close is blocked for scope drift, evidence mismatch, lineage mismatch, method/factor mismatch, unresolved material QMS or custody exceptions, missing assurance/claim authorization, surface divergence, unresolved material control failure, missing authority or attempted silent overwrite.

## Boundaries
Period close does not create observations, establish environmental truth, constitute regulatory approval, certify product quality, award funding, or release physical product.
