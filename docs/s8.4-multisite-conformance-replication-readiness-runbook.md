# S8.4 — Multisite Conformance and Replication Readiness

## Objective
Establish whether the S8.1–S8.3 control system can be replicated at additional admitted sites while preserving evidence integrity, local authorization, DMRV semantics, QMS/custody controls and public-claim boundaries.

## Replication rule
A site is a separate evidence domain. Replication copies the **control framework**, not environmental observations or conclusions.

## Procedure
1. Select a candidate site and establish an independent site identity.
2. Verify local authorization and accountable operator.
3. Instantiate the S8.2 operational-readiness controls.
4. Verify site-specific collector/equipment and physical identity.
5. Verify rain-event, first-flush, collection, sampling and custody procedures.
6. Verify the applicable QMS/laboratory pathway.
7. Instantiate the DMRV profile without silently changing evidence-class semantics.
8. Reconcile site-specific evidence manifests and source provenance.
9. Verify anchor compatibility where anchoring is required.
10. Compare control conformance across sites without treating values as interchangeable.
11. Review deviations, CAPA, rollback and exception handling.
12. Review public/investor claims for site-specific scope.
13. Issue an authorized replication decision.

## Decision semantics

**READY_FOR_REPLICATION** means the control framework is ready to be instantiated at the reviewed site scope. It does not establish that rain occurred, water was collected, water quality passed, premium conditions were met, or regulatory/product release occurred.

**CONDITIONAL_REPLICATION** requires explicitly recorded conditions and owners before the next execution gate.

**HOLD / NONCONFORMING** prevents promotion until material deficiencies are resolved.

## Cross-site evidence boundary

Never:
- copy observations from one site into another;
- treat administrative coverage as physical installations;
- convert forecast/model/satellite/proxy data into observed measurements;
- use an anchor as proof of environmental truth;
- infer product or regulatory release from replication readiness.
