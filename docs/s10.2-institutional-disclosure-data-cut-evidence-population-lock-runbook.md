# S10.2 — Institutional Disclosure Data Cut and Evidence Population Lock

## Purpose
S10.2 establishes the reproducible evidence population used for a reporting period. The lock separates what was available and admissible at the cut from later corrections, additions or reinterpretations.

## Sequence
PERIOD SCOPE CONFIRMATION → SOURCE REGISTER FREEZE → DATA CUT → INCLUSION/EXCLUSION RECONCILIATION → EVIDENCE POPULATION DIGEST → LOCK AUTHORIZATION → DOWNSTREAM HANDOFF.

## Population record
Each locked population records period_id, population_id, cut timestamp, boundary version, methodology versions, source-register version, included/excluded evidence references, accountable owner, reconciliation status and lock authority.

## Evidence rules
An evidence reference must be stable, attributable and period/boundary aware. Evidence classes remain distinct: observation, forecast, model output, satellite, calculated, proxy and disclosure-derived material must not be silently substituted for one another.

Missing data is not zero. A forecast is not an observation. An administrative reporting node is not a physical harvest site. A blockchain anchor demonstrates integrity of the referenced record, not environmental truth.

## Late changes
After EVIDENCE_LOCKED, no silent insertion or mutation is permitted. A material late source, correction or reinterpretation creates a successor population linked to the predecessor, with reason, scope/period impact, source/methodology impact and renewed reconciliation/authorization.

## Fail closed
Block lock or downstream handoff when period/boundary is undefined, source register is missing, references are unstable, duplicates are unresolved, material inclusion/exclusion disputes remain, population digest does not reconcile, or lock authority is absent.

## Physical boundary
This control freezes reporting evidence; it does not assert that rain occurred, water was harvested, a batch exists, laboratory results exist, premium conditions were met or a product was released.
