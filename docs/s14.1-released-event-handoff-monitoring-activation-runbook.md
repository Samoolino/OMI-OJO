# S14.1 — Released Event Handoff and Monitoring Activation

## Purpose
Establish the controlled handoff from an actual S13.14 release decision into S14 monitoring.

S14.1 does not authorize a product release. It only activates monitoring after a valid release record exists.

## Sequence
1. Receive the actual S13.14 release decision.
2. Reconcile release authority, scope, effective date and population.
3. Reconcile event, batch, container and seal population.
4. Transfer the evidence lineage and package digest.
5. Transfer QMS, laboratory and custody status.
6. Transfer methodology, boundary and factor versions.
7. Transfer authorized claims and product/label surfaces.
8. Transfer applicable regulatory conditions and expiry controls.
9. Assign monitoring owner and escalation path.
10. Capture a baseline snapshot of the handed-off state.
11. Activate the monitoring window.
12. Lock the handoff record.

## Activation conditions
Monitoring may activate only when every required input is reconciled. Missing authorization, population mismatch, incomplete lineage, unresolved QMS/custody status, claim divergence, regulatory uncertainty or missing monitoring ownership causes a fail-closed HOLD.

## Monitoring boundary
The baseline is a record of the authorized state at handoff. It is not a new environmental observation.

Monitoring alerts are control events. They do not automatically alter product quality, regulatory status or environmental metrics.
