# OMI-OJO — Institutional Business Logic v2

**Status:** Investment-grade scope / M-1 controlled infrastructure validation  
**Reference deployment:** Lagos, Nigeria  
**Expansion:** Europe + Caucasus + Central Asia  
**Commercial principle:** Evidence infrastructure first; environmental-market readiness as a premium downstream layer.

## 1. Business thesis

OMI-OJO is a climate-water intelligence, measurement and digital MRV infrastructure business. It converts distributed environmental activity into structured, traceable and institutionally reviewable evidence.

The company monetizes the evidence chain—not an unverified environmental claim.

`DATA → MEASUREMENT → QC → RECONCILIATION → VALIDATION → REVIEW → VERIFICATION → REPORTING → INTEGRITY → SCALE`

## 2. Four business planes

### Plane A — Data & Intelligence
Environmental providers, rainfall/forecast data, satellite inputs, site data, analytics, collection modelling, water-efficiency and climate-risk intelligence.

### Plane B — Measurement & Infrastructure
OMI-OJO Nodes, telemetry, rainfall and water measurements, event records, sampling/custody, QMS interfaces and physical asset records.

### Plane C — Verification & Institutional Reporting
Evidence objects, deterministic evidence packages, reconciliation, reviewer workflows, DMRV status, ESG/GHG reporting, investor/VDR reporting, regulatory evidence and integrity anchoring.

### Plane D — Premium Environmental-Market Evidence
Enhanced measurement continuity, QMS/water-quality evidence, baseline/additionality evidence management, methodology-ready packaging and independent-review coordination for projects that may pursue eligible environmental-credit or climate-finance pathways.

Plane D is an add-on. It does not convert activity data into credits automatically.

## 4. Customer-to-product logic

**Municipality / infrastructure operator** → Nodes + OS + institutional reporting.  
**Corporate / campus / hospitality** → Nodes + water analytics + ESG reporting.  
**Industrial / agricultural operator** → measurement + efficiency analytics + MRV.  
**Climate programme / development funder** → project monitoring + evidence room + milestone reporting.  
**Project developer / climate-finance sponsor** → full MRV + Premium methodology-readiness.  
**Verification/data partner** → evidence interface + deterministic package + review workflow.

## 5. Measurement-to-value contract

Every material record carries, where applicable:

- evidence ID;
- project/site/node ID;
- timestamp;
- geographic reference;
- variable/value/unit;
- source/provider;
- instrument/acquisition method;
- methodology/version;
- evidence class;
- QC/reconciliation status;
- uncertainty/assumption reference;
- parent/derived evidence references;
- evidence/package hash;
- reviewer status;
- release status.

Evidence classes remain explicit: `OBSERVED | FORECAST | MODELLED | SATELLITE | CALCULATED | PROXY`.

## 6. Product proof ladder

**P0 — Concept proof:** architecture, hypothesis, assumptions.  
**P1 — Technical proof:** schemas, APIs, calculations, fixtures, deterministic packages.  
**P2 — Field/product proof:** authorized physical measurements and reproducible operations.  
**P3 — Institutional proof:** required technical/QMS review, independent verification and release.

Investor materials must label claims by the highest achieved proof layer.

## 7. Claim-state machine

`DESIGNED → IMPLEMENTED → INTEGRATION_READY → CONTROLLED_TEST → REPORTABLE → VALIDATED → VERIFIED → ANCHORED → RELEASED`

A blockchain anchor proves integrity of a defined evidence package; it does not prove physical truth, additionality, methodology eligibility or credit issuance.

## 8. ESG/GHG reporting product

The reporting layer separates:

1. source evidence;
2. measured activity;
3. calculated indicators;
4. assumptions and uncertainty;
5. methodology/version;
6. reviewer status;
7. integrity proof;
8. release/acceptance state.

The system therefore supports an institutional path from source data to report without collapsing forecast, measurement, calculation and verification into one number.

## 9. Premium Carbon Evidence Amplification

Premium is positioned as **evidence amplification**, not carbon-credit creation.

Premium can add:

- higher-frequency telemetry;
- additional sensor classes;
- water-quality/QMS evidence;
- environmental indicator library;
- baseline/additionality evidence management;
- methodology-ready calculation inputs;
- independent-review coordination;
- registry/standard preparation where applicable;
- enhanced climate-finance/investor evidence rooms.

Carbon pathway:

`CONCEPT → ACTIVITY DATA → METHODOLOGY ELIGIBILITY → BASELINE/ADDITIONALITY → MONITORING → CALCULATION → VALIDATION → VERIFICATION → ISSUANCE → RETIREMENT`

No external communication may skip these states.

## 10. Carbon business boundary

OMI-OJO may generate carbon-relevant activity data and methodology-ready evidence. It must not represent water captured, stored or reused as a verified carbon credit without a qualifying methodology, defensible baseline/counterfactual, monitoring, validation, verification and registry issuance. ## 12. Investment narrative

The investable asset is the reusable evidence spine across climate-water projects. Each additional site can reuse the software, schemas, evidence semantics and reviewer workflow while localizing sources, instruments, regulations and methodologies.

**Moat:** interoperable measurement + evidence lineage + verification workflow + institutional reporting + integrity anchoring.

**Expansion:** Lagos is the empirical reference environment; Europe, Azerbaijan/Caucasus and Central Asia are separate deployment contexts with independent evidence.

## 13. Non-negotiable claims policy

Do not claim:

- certified carbon credits without issuance evidence;
- verified impact from blockchain alone;
- measured rainfall from forecast data;
- physical production because software exists;
- revenue that has not been generated;
- potable water without treatment/laboratory/QMS/regulatory evidence;
- transferred evidence values between geographies.
