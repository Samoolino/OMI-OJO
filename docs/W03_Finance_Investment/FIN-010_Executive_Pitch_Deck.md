# FIN-010 — Executive Pitch Deck Source

**State:** S2 — SPECIFIED

## Investment narrative

Blue-Ether develops distributed water and environmental-data infrastructure for urban environments, combining harvesting hardware, telemetry, quality controls, predictive rainfall intelligence and auditable DMRV.

## Problem

Urban water resilience, environmental monitoring and climate reporting remain fragmented, with weak links between physical infrastructure and verifiable data.

## Solution

A networked pod architecture converts local environmental conditions into measurable water, operational data and auditable environmental evidence.

## Defensibility

- event-level physical harvesting records;
- telemetry and forecast history;
- QMS evidence;
- DMRV data lineage;
- operational logistics network;
- version-controlled calculation methods.
