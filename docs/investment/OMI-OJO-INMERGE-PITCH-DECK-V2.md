## Slide 2 — Institutional problem
Climate-water projects produce data, infrastructure and reports, but these layers are often disconnected. Investors, operators and institutions need traceable evidence linking physical activity to reported numbers.

**Gap:** no common evidence spine from source and measurement through review, reporting and institutional acceptance.

## Slide 3 — Solution
OMI-OJO is the evidence and intelligence layer connecting climate data, field telemetry, physical water assets, technical measurement, DMRV, ESG/GHG reporting and integrity anchoring.

It complements—not replaces—sensors, laboratories, regulators and independent verifiers.

## Slide 4 — Business logic
`SOURCE → MEASURE → QC → RECONCILE → VALIDATE → REVIEW → VERIFY → ANCHOR → REPORT → ACCEPT → SUBSCRIBE / SCALE`

The product is the **evidence spine**, not a dashboard alone.

## Slide 5 — Product suite
**OS:** evidence registry, ingestion, DMRV, reviewer workflow, reporting.

**Nodes:** hardware-agnostic measurement/telemetry interface.

**Intelligence:** rainfall, collection, water-efficiency, drought/stormwater and climate-risk analytics.

**MRV:** deterministic evidence packages, reconciliation, review and integrity proofs.

**Institutional:** ESG/GHG, VDR, funder, operator and regulatory reporting.

**Premium:** enhanced measurement/QMS and methodology-ready environmental/carbon evidence.

## Slide 6 — Technical measurement
Each material evidence object contains project/site/node ID, timestamp, geographic reference, variable/value/unit, source/instrument, methodology/version, QC/reconciliation state, uncertainty/assumptions, parent evidence, package hash, reviewer status and release status.

Evidence classes: `OBSERVED | FORECAST | MODELLED | SATELLITE | CALCULATED | PROXY`.

## Slide 7 — Proof architecture
**P0 Concept:** architecture, hypothesis, assumptions.

**P1 Technical:** schemas, APIs, calculations, controlled tests.

**P2 Field/Product:** authorized physical measurements and reproducible operations.

**P3 Institutional:** technical/QMS review, independent verification and release.

Status contract: `DESIGNED → IMPLEMENTED → INTEGRATION_READY → CONTROLLED_TEST → REPORTABLE → VALIDATED → VERIFIED → ANCHORED → RELEASED`.
