# S10.6 — Institutional Environmental Evidence Assurance and Exception Disposition

## Purpose
S10.6 evaluates the reconciled environmental evidence population and records assurance disposition. It controls exceptions and claim scope without converting software status into environmental truth.

## Sequence
EVIDENCE RECONCILIATION INTAKE → ASSURANCE CRITERIA CHECK → SOURCE/PROVENANCE REVIEW → QMS/CUSTODY REVIEW → DMRV LINEAGE REVIEW → METHOD/BOUNDARY REVIEW → EXCEPTION CLASSIFICATION → DISPOSITION AUTHORIZATION → CLAIM SCOPE REVIEW → ASSURANCE HANDOFF.

## Assurance domains
Site/event identity; observation provenance; sample/custody/QMS; batch/seal linkage; DMRV lineage; GHG/ESG methodology; source health; anchor integrity; claim scope; exceptions/CAPA.

## Assurance outcomes
- ACCEPTED_FOR_REVIEW
- ACCEPTED_WITH_LIMITATIONS
- CONDITIONAL
- HOLD
- REJECTED

These are assurance workflow outcomes, not environmental certification statuses.

## Exception lifecycle
DETECTED → CLASSIFIED → CONTAINED → INVESTIGATING → CAPA_REQUIRED → REVIEW_PENDING → CLOSED.

Material issues may be REOPENED or SUSPENDED. Closure requires evidence of disposition appropriate to severity.

## Fail closed
Block assurance handoff when material exceptions lack disposition, evidence identity conflicts, period/boundary mismatches exist, evidence classes are promoted incorrectly, QMS/custody lineage is broken, methodology is unresolved, material limitations are omitted, claims exceed reviewed evidence, or reviewer authority is missing.

## Claim boundary
An assurance disposition is scoped to the reviewed population, period, methodology and evidence. It does not establish regulatory approval, certification, premium status or product release.

## Physical boundary
No empirical value is created by S10.6. Environmental evidence must originate from controlled empirical operations and retain its provenance.
