# S10.1 — Institutional Disclosure Period Operations and Assurance Calendar

## Purpose
S10.1 turns the S10 operating model into a recurring calendar control. It defines reporting periods, cutoffs, assurance windows, owners, evidence-population locks and closeout rules without treating calendar state as environmental evidence.

## Period types
- MONTHLY_OPERATIONAL
- QUARTERLY_ESG
- ANNUAL_CLIMATE
- EVENT_DRIVEN
- RESTATEMENT

## Controlled sequence
CALENDAR APPROVAL → PERIOD OPEN → BOUNDARY LOCK → DATA CUT → EVIDENCE POPULATION LOCK → DMRV RECONCILIATION → GHG METHOD CHECK → QMS/CUSTODY/EXCEPTION REVIEW → ASSURANCE WINDOW → DISCLOSURE REVIEW → AUTHORIZATION → PUBLICATION → PERIOD CLOSE.

## Required calendar record
Each period records a stable period_id, type, start/end dates, data-cut deadline, assurance window, disclosure-authorization deadline, publication target, accountable owners, boundary version, methodology versions, evidence-population identifier and state.

## Cutoff discipline
After DATA_CUT and EVIDENCE_POPULATION_LOCK, changes must enter controlled change/restatement governance. A late source or correction is not silently inserted into a locked population.

## Assurance calendar
The assurance window identifies the review population and period. It does not itself constitute independent assurance. Reviewer authority, independence/conflict status, methodology, sampling and limitations remain governed by S9.4/S9.5.

## Fail-closed rules
Block period close or publication when dates/owners/boundaries are incomplete, the evidence population changes after lock, material reconciliation or exceptions remain unresolved, methodology versions are missing, assurance scope is absent, authorization is missing, or the published surface differs from the authorized pack.

## Environmental evidence boundary
A calendar state is operational metadata. It is not evidence that rain occurred, water was collected, a batch exists, quality was measured, premium conditions were achieved, or a product was released.
