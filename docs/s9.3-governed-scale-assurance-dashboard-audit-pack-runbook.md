# S9.3 — Governed Scale Assurance Dashboard and Audit Pack

## Objective
Create an investor/public-safe assurance surface that makes climate and ESG data conformity inspectable without overstating what the evidence proves.

The dashboard is a control and disclosure-preparation surface. It is not a certification engine.

## Standards-aware conformity basis
- IFRS S2 Climate-related Disclosures — governance, strategy, risk management, metrics and targets; climate metrics include Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3 GHG emissions and require the measurement approach, inputs and assumptions to be disclosed.
- GHG Protocol — organizational and operational boundaries, Scope 1/2/3 accounting, activity data, emission factors, calculation methods and double-counting controls.
- ESRS E1 — mapped as a jurisdiction-aware disclosure profile. Current applicable requirements must be resolved by reporting jurisdiction. Any 2026 EFRAG draft datapoint material is treated as non-authoritative supporting material until final publication.

These are alignment/mapping profiles, not claims of certification or compliance.

## UX conformity contract
Every quantitative climate/ESG metric must expose:
1. metric identifier and human-readable name;
2. reporting entity/site and boundary;
3. reporting period;
4. value and unit;
5. data/evidence class;
6. source and provenance;
7. methodology/version;
8. quality/reconciliation status;
9. uncertainty or estimation method where applicable;
10. evidence references;
11. review/assurance state.

The interface must visually distinguish:
SIGNAL → REPORTABLE → VALIDATED → VERIFIED → ANCHORED → ASSURANCE REVIEW

A higher software state never automatically proves environmental truth.

## Audit-pack structure
The pack contains:
- control effectiveness matrix;
- exception register;
- evidence lineage;
- DMRV reconciliation;
- QMS/custody status;
- anchor register;
- change/version register;
- CAPA register;
- independent review log;
- claim review;
- standards mapping ledger.

## Climate/GHG controls
For GHG metrics, retain:
- organizational boundary;
- operational boundary;
- Scope 1 / Scope 2 / Scope 3 classification;
- activity data;
- emission factor and source;
- calculation method;
- estimation/uncertainty information;
- base year/restatement information where applicable;
- methodology version;
- evidence references.

Missing context blocks a disclosure-ready status.

## Data quality rules
- Missing data is not zero.
- Forecast is not observation.
- Model output is not measured telemetry.
- Satellite/model evidence remains labelled by class.
- Aggregate claims preserve source lineage.
- Administrative coverage is not physical harvest.
- Blockchain anchoring proves integrity of the referenced package, not environmental truth.

## Assurance rules
The dashboard may show control status, evidence completeness, exception severity/state, DMRV reconciliation, reviewer scope and period, claim-support status, and standards mapping status.

It must not show an unsupported certified, compliant, verified impact, premium, regulatory approved or product released state.

Material unresolved exceptions block claim promotion.

## Fail-closed conditions
HOLD or INCOMPLETE where evidence references are missing, lineage is broken, material exceptions remain unresolved, reviewer authority/conflict record is absent, the review period is stale, a GHG scope is asserted without boundary/method context, methodology versions are incompatible without change control, or an unsupported claim is promoted.

## Current physical boundary
Physical/product status remains NOT_RELEASED. No dashboard state may be interpreted as evidence that rain was harvested, water was bottled, laboratory results exist, premium status was established, or a regulatory/product release occurred.
