# S7.3 — Independent Audit and Effectiveness Assurance Runbook

## Objective
Provide independent assurance over the evidence chain, monitoring/reconciliation controls and corrective-action effectiveness for a defined scope.

## Preconditions
1. S7.1 reconciliation and S7.2 CAPA records are available where applicable.
2. Audit scope and criteria are defined.
3. Auditor independence is declared.
4. Sampling methodology and limitations can be recorded.

## Procedure
1. Create a unique audit ID.
2. Freeze audit scope, period and criteria revision.
3. Record auditor identity and independence declaration.
4. Record sample-selection method and the records examined.
5. Test release, batch/seal, DMRV, anchor, monitoring and CAPA controls within scope.
6. Record each finding against a criterion and evidence reference.
7. Identify affected scope and required owner/due date for actionable findings.
8. Assess CAPA effectiveness against predefined criteria.
9. Record limitations and sampling boundaries.
10. Escalate material findings to HOLD or corrective action as required.
11. Record assurance status and integrity digest.
12. Preserve the audit trail without rewriting underlying evidence.

## Assurance semantics
**ASSURANCE_COMPLETE** means the defined audit procedures were completed and the recorded conclusion is supported by the evidence examined. It does not mean universal compliance or absence of all future issues.

## Fail-closed controls
No assurance completion when independence, scope, criteria, sampling records or evidence are missing. Material findings cannot be silently closed.

## Claim boundary
An independent audit is a bounded assurance activity. It does not replace regulatory authorization, product release authority, laboratory evidence or environmental verification beyond its stated scope.
