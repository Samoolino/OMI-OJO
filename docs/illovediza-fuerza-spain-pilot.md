# Illovediza Fuerza — Spain Secondary Institutional Pilot

## 1. Position in the programme

**Illovediza Fuerza** is a secondary country pilot of Blue-Ether OS / OMI-OJO. It runs alongside the Global Lagos → Dubai programme and reuses the institutional evidence semantics, DMRV controls, ESG/GHG methodology boundaries, verification ladder and blockchain integrity model.

It does **not** inherit measurements, water-quality results, coordinates, field claims or verification outcomes from Lagos or any other country.

## 2. Geographic coverage

The Spain registry is designed for the whole national administrative hierarchy:

`Spain → Autonomous Community/City → Province → Municipality → Authorized Field Site → Observation Node`

The 2026 INE reference lists **8,132 municipalities**, with **17 autonomous communities and 2 autonomous cities**, across **50 provinces**. The registry must remain synchronized with INE/Registro de Entidades Locales updates rather than treating a static count as permanently authoritative.

Authoritative administrative sources:
- INE municipality and code registry
- Spanish public administration autonomous-community directory
- authoritative public GIS for geometry and administrative boundaries

## 3. Node principle

Every municipality gets a **reporting node**, not a claim of physical infrastructure.

A node can initially contain:
- administrative identity and code;
- authoritative boundary reference;
- source/provider registry;
- rainfall and weather reporting availability;
- forecast/model availability;
- atmospheric and remote-sensing availability;
- evidence readiness;
- DMRV readiness;
- ESG/GHG reporting readiness;
- institutional engagement status.

A physical collector, sensor, laboratory, bottle/batch or harvest event only appears after authorized field implementation.

## 4. Reporting objectives

Illovediza Fuerza reports the same core environmental evidence categories used by the parent programme:

### Rain and water
- rainfall observations;
- precipitation forecasts;
- antecedent rainfall;
- atmospheric moisture/humidity;
- rain-event detection;
- collection opportunity;
- first-flush records;
- collection volumes when physically measured;
- water-quality/QMS evidence when sampled.

### Atmosphere and environment
- temperature;
- relative humidity;
- wind;
- PM2.5/PM10;
- NO2/SO2/O3/CO where supported;
- aerosol/remote sensing;
- solar-radiation indicators;
- other reportable environmental indicators added through controlled registry changes.

### ESG/GHG
Each material indicator must retain its source, methodology, units, temporal/spatial boundary, calculation logic, uncertainty/limitations and review state.

## 5. Evidence model

The Spain pilot inherits the parent evidence lifecycle:

`RAW → INGESTED → QUALITY_CHECKED → VALIDATED → PACKAGED → REVIEWED`

Evidence classes remain:

`OBSERVED | FORECAST | MODELLED | SATELLITE | CALCULATED | PROXY`

Verification remains:

`REPORTABLE → VALIDATED → VERIFIED → ANCHORED`

The project must never promote forecast, modelled, calculated or proxy information to measured evidence without the required evidence.

## 6. Non-contradiction

Spain nodes operate under the same institutional non-contradiction rule:

> Compatible time + space + units + methodology + provenance + quality are required before sources can support a common claim.

Material conflicts fail closed. Sources are not blindly averaged. Corrections are append-only successor records.

## 7. Premium RainWater boundary

The pilot can report **rainwater opportunity** before physical harvest.

It cannot call a water batch Premium RainWater merely because:
- it is in Spain;
- rainfall was high;
- a forecast was favorable;
- atmospheric conditions were favorable;
- blockchain evidence exists; or
- a municipality has a reporting node.

Premium classification requires the physical evidence chain:

`Authorized Site → Rain Event → First Flush → Controlled Collection → Sampling/Custody → QMS/Lab → Batch → Seal → DMRV Review → Regulatory/Product Release`

## 8. Blockchain

Blockchain is an integrity layer only.

The Spain pilot may anchor deterministic evidence roots and expose a verifier showing network, transaction, block/time and evidence-root linkage. It does not prove water quality, environmental truth, legal compliance or grant eligibility.

## 9. Institutional engagement

The Spain pilot is designed to support engagement with:
- public administrations;
- municipalities;
- environmental and water institutions;
- universities and research organizations;
- technology/building partners;
- ESG/impact programmes;
- grant makers;
- institutional investors after appropriate diligence.

Funding requests should be converted into measurable work packages with owners, deliverables, budgets, evidence and acceptance criteria.

## 11. Institutional claim boundary

**Designed coverage is not deployment.**

**Reporting readiness is not measured evidence.**

**Measured evidence is not automatically verified.**

**Blockchain anchoring is not environmental verification.**

**A node is not a harvest site.**

**A rainfall forecast is not a rainfall measurement.**

**Premium RainWater remains a controlled physical/QMS/product-release determination.**

## 12. Relationship to Global Lagos → Dubai

The two programmes are complementary:

- **Global Lagos → Dubai:** reference/proof pilot and cross-continental network architecture.
- **Illovediza Fuerza:** Spain-wide institutional replication and municipal reporting grid.

Together they demonstrate that the evidence architecture can scale geographically while maintaining a single non-contradictory methodology.
