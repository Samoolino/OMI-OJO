# S10.7 — Institutional Disclosure Claim Authorization and Public Surface Reconciliation

## Purpose
S10.7 controls promotion of reviewed evidence into authorized disclosure claims and reconciles the public and investor surfaces to the same authorized record.

## Sequence
DISCLOSURE SCOPE FREEZE → ASSURANCE CONCLUSION CHECK → CLAIM REGISTER RECONCILIATION → EVIDENCE SCOPE CHECK → LIMITATION/EXCEPTION CHECK → AUTHORITY REVIEW → CLAIM PROMOTION AUTHORIZATION → PUBLIC SURFACE RECONCILIATION → INVESTOR SURFACE RECONCILIATION → POST-PUBLICATION INTEGRITY CHECK.

## Claim states
DRAFT → EVIDENCE_MAPPED → ASSURANCE_REVIEWED → CONDITIONALLY_AUTHORIZED → AUTHORIZED.

Controlled terminal states are BLOCKED, WITHDRAWN and SUPERSEDED.

## Required claim record
Every authorized claim retains claim ID, text, entity/boundary, reporting period, methodology version, evidence references, assurance reference, limitations, exception references, authorization authority/timestamp, surface references and predecessor linkage where applicable.

## Fail closed
Block authorization where evidence references are absent, claim scope exceeds assurance scope, period or methodology differs, material exceptions remain unresolved, limitations are omitted, authority is missing, or public/investor surfaces diverge from the authorized record.

A claim must not imply environmental certification, regulatory approval, premium product status, funding award or product release unless separately evidenced and authorized under its own applicable control.

## Surface reconciliation
Public and investor representations must resolve to the same authorized claim record. Changes require controlled successor records; historical records are never silently overwritten.

## Non-equivalences
- Disclosure authorization ≠ certification.
- Authorized claim ≠ new observation.
- Public disclosure ≠ product release.
- Investor disclosure ≠ independent assurance.
- Anchor ≠ environmental truth.

## Physical boundary
S10.7 creates no empirical environmental measurement and does not authorize physical/product release. Current physical status remains NOT_RELEASED.
