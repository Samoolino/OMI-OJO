# S9.6 — Governed Scale Disclosure Assurance and Restatement Control

## Objective
S9.6 controls the lifecycle after a disclosure has been reviewed: change detection, impact assessment, correction/restatement decisions, successor records, reauthorization and public/investor reconciliation.

## Controlled sequence
PERIOD AND SCOPE FREEZE → DISCLOSURE POPULATION SNAPSHOT → ASSURANCE EVIDENCE RECONCILIATION → CHANGE IMPACT ASSESSMENT → MATERIALITY REVIEW → RESTATEMENT DECISION → SUCCESSOR RECORD CREATION → DISCLOSURE REAUTHORIZATION → PUBLIC/INVESTOR RECONCILIATION → PERIOD CLOSE.

## Non-destructive history
Historical evidence and published records are never overwritten. Material changes create linked successor records with predecessor references, reason, affected scope/period, methodology version and authorizing review.

## Change classes
SOURCE_CHANGE; METHODOLOGY_CHANGE; BOUNDARY_CHANGE; CALCULATION_CHANGE; EVIDENCE_REINTERPRETATION; PERIOD_CORRECTION; DISCLOSURE_WORDING_CHANGE; REGULATORY_REQUIREMENT_CHANGE.

## Assurance outputs
UNCHANGED; CORRECTED; RESTATED; WITHDRAWN; HOLD.

## Climate / ESG control
Disclosure records remain traceable to the applicable reporting framework, methodology version, evidence class, boundary and period. A restatement is a controlled reporting action, not a new environmental observation.

## Fail closed
Hold publication when material change impact is unassessed, authority is missing, predecessor/successor linkage is broken, historical state was overwritten, scope/period differs from assurance, methodology cannot be reproduced, a material exception remains unresolved, or public/investor surfaces diverge from the authorized record.

## Physical boundary
NOT_RELEASED remains unchanged. Restatement/disclosure assurance does not establish harvest, bottling, water quality, premium status, regulatory approval or product release.
