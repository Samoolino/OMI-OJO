# OMI-OJO Investment VDR

This directory defines the investment-grade virtual data room (VDR) evidence structure for OMI-OJO.

## Evidence rooms

- `00_INVESTOR_INDEX` — investment thesis, executive summary, data-room index.
- `01_CORPORATE_LEGAL` — entity, ownership, governance, IP and material legal records.
- `02_PRODUCT_AND_DAPP` — product architecture, DMRV, APIs, security and deployment evidence.
- `03_ESG_AND_ENVIRONMENT` — ESG framework, environmental baseline, water, climate and GHG evidence.
- `04_DMRV_EVIDENCE` — collection points, collectors, events, samples, provenance, hashes and verification.
- `05_GHG_AND_CLIMATE` — organizational/operational boundaries, activity data, calculations and assumptions.
- `06_PHYSICAL_INFRASTRUCTURE` — rainfall collector, first flush, sample capsule, pouch/bottle and material QA.
- `07_MARKET_AND_COMMERCIAL` — customers, pilots, contracts, pricing, pipeline and competition.
- `08_FINANCIAL` — historicals, model, runway, unit economics, valuation and use of funds.
- `09_RISK_AND_GOVERNANCE` — enterprise, climate, data, cyber, smart-contract, measurement and greenwashing risk.
- `10_INVESTMENT` — fundraising memo, SAFE term sheet, cap table, dilution and closing checklist.

## Evidence status

`DRAFT → EVIDENCE_PENDING → EVIDENCE_CAPTURED → DMRV_VALIDATED → VERIFIER_REVIEW → VERIFIED → INVESTOR_READY`

A claim must never become `VERIFIED` merely because it was uploaded. Modeled, measured, calculated and verified evidence remain distinct.

## Environmental evidence rule

Open-Meteo/reanalysis observations are modeled contextual evidence. Physical rain-gauge or collector observations are measured evidence. Laboratory results are verified measurement evidence only after the applicable QA/QC process.

## Legal note

The VDR is an operational diligence framework, not legal advice. Securities documents must be reviewed and executed under the law applicable to the issuer and investor.
