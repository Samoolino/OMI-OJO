# OMI-OJO — EuroAsia Business Scope & Investment Logic

**Edition:** 2026
**Baseline:** M-1 — Investor-Ready Evidence Core
**Reference deployment:** Lagos, Nigeria
**Expansion:** Azerbaijan/Caucasus → Europe → Central Asia

## 1. Strategic definition

OMI-OJO is a climate-water intelligence, measurement and digital MRV infrastructure platform. It converts distributed water-resilience activity into traceable evidence for ESG/GHG reporting, institutional decision-making and, where eligible, environmental-market/climate-finance pathways.

**Positioning:** Built from Lagos. Designed for EuroAsia. Structured for global deployment.

The company does not sell a carbon-credit promise. It sells the infrastructure that makes environmental activity measurable, reviewable and finance-ready.

## 2. Business planes

1. **Data & Intelligence** — weather/environmental data, forecasting, modelling, water-efficiency and climate-risk analytics.
2. **Measurement & Infrastructure** — hardware-agnostic OMI-OJO Nodes, telemetry, water measurements, QMS and field operations.
3. **Verification & Reporting** — evidence registry, deterministic packages, reconciliation, DMRV, ESG/GHG reports, VDR and institutional review workflows.
4. **Premium Environmental-Market Evidence** — enhanced evidence continuity, methodology-ready carbon-relevant data and climate-finance support.

Planes 1–3 are the core recurring business. Plane 4 is an optional premium expansion.

## 3. Product architecture

### OMI-OJO OS
Evidence registry, source/provider registry, telemetry ingestion, QC, reconciliation, DMRV workflow, reviewer workflow, public verification surface and institutional reporting.

### OMI-OJO Nodes
Hardware-agnostic interface for rainfall, water-volume, storage, event and environmental measurements. Instrument identity and calibration/QMS records are first-class evidence.

### OMI-OJO Intelligence
Forecast ingestion, observed-vs-forecast separation, rainfall/event classification, catchment modelling, storage planning, water-efficiency, drought/stormwater and climate-risk indicators.

### OMI-OJO MRV
Evidence classes, deterministic evidence packages, reviewer states, SHA-256 evidence roots and optional blockchain integrity anchors.

### OMI-OJO Institutional
ESG/GHG reporting, funder milestone reporting, investor/VDR evidence rooms, operator/QMS records and regulatory evidence packages.

### OMI-OJO Premium
Enhanced telemetry, water-quality/QMS evidence, expanded environmental indicators, baseline/additionality evidence management, methodology-ready packaging, verification coordination and climate-finance evidence.

## 4. Measurement-to-value chain

`SOURCE/SITE AUTHORIZATION → OBSERVATION → TECHNICAL MEASUREMENT → QC → RECONCILIATION → VALIDATION → INDEPENDENT REVIEW → VERIFICATION → INTEGRITY ANCHOR → INSTITUTIONAL REPORT → CUSTOMER ACCEPTANCE → RECURRING SERVICE / SCALE`

The platform explicitly distinguishes:

`REPORTABLE ≠ MEASURED ≠ VALIDATED ≠ VERIFIED ≠ ANCHORED ≠ RELEASED`

## 7. Premium Carbon Evidence Amplification

Premium increases the depth, continuity and reviewability of evidence. It can add:

`ENHANCED MEASUREMENT → PROVENANCE → QMS → BASELINE → ADDITIONALITY → MONITORING → CALCULATION LINEAGE → REVIEW`

It cannot bypass:

`METHODOLOGY ELIGIBILITY → VALIDATION → VERIFICATION → ISSUANCE → RETIREMENT`

Therefore:

**Carbon-relevant data is not a carbon credit.**

Any carbon/environmental-credit claim requires an applicable methodology, project boundary, baseline/counterfactual, additionality where required, monitoring plan, calculations, uncertainty/leakage/double-counting controls, validation/verification and registry treatment as applicable.

No revenue or valuation assumption should depend on unissued credits.

## 8. EuroAsia deployment logic

### Lagos / Nigeria — reference proof
Rainfall, collection, urban resilience, physical measurement and evidence validation.

### Azerbaijan / Caucasus — gateway deployment
Climate-water resilience, corporate/institutional pilots, measurement infrastructure and regional partnerships.

### Europe — water-smart deployment
Water efficiency, rain/stormwater management, reuse, smart measurement and institutional ESG reporting.

### Central Asia — scarcity/resilience deployment
Drought intelligence, irrigation/storage evidence, water-efficiency analytics and resilience planning.

Only technology contracts, schemas and evidence semantics are reusable. Measurement values, regulatory interpretations and verification outcomes are always local.

## 9. Institutional technical stack

**Data:** weather/environmental APIs, satellite/open data, site observations and telemetry.

**Edge:** hardware-agnostic node interface, instrument identity, event records and measurement/QMS records.

**Intelligence:** forecast ingestion, observed-vs-forecast reconciliation, catchment/storage models, water-efficiency and climate-risk analytics.

**Evidence:** canonical evidence schema, provenance, QC, reconciliation, deterministic package generation and status machine.

**Integrity:** SHA-256 evidence/package roots and blockchain anchoring; raw telemetry remains off-chain.

**Reporting:** public-safe dashboard, ESG/GHG reports, investor/VDR room and regulatory packages.

**Governance:** role-based review, claim controls, audit lineage, security/privacy review and fail-closed release gates.

## 11. Investment thesis

The investable asset is the evidence infrastructure connecting **climate-water activity to institutional trust**. The defensibility is the combined evidence spine:

`DATA → MEASUREMENT → RECONCILIATION → DMRV → REVIEW → INTEGRITY → REPORTING → SCALE`

Premium creates a higher-value downstream service without turning speculative carbon issuance into the core business.

## 12. Current boundary

The repository is at M-1 Investor-Ready Evidence Core. The architecture and software/evidence foundation are defined, while controlled live provider integrations, empirical field telemetry, QMS/water-quality release, independent verification, physical production and commercial revenue remain gated until acceptance evidence exists.

The 400 L/pod/month value remains a financial-model assumption, not a certified production result.

## 13. Institutional claim policy

Do not claim:

- certified carbon credits without an applicable methodology and issuance pathway;
- verified environmental performance from blockchain anchoring alone;
- measured rainfall from forecast data;
- physical production because software exists;
- potable water without treatment/laboratory/QMS/regulatory evidence;
- revenue that has not been generated;
- evidence from another geography as local evidence.
