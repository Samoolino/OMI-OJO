# S13.13 — Premium-Condition Decision

## Purpose
Apply the approved Premium RainWater condition criteria to the exact evidence population reviewed through S13.12.

This gate makes a controlled eligibility decision; it does not create environmental observations.

## Preconditions
- S13.12 external assurance review is concluded within the required scope.
- Premium-condition criteria and version are frozen.
- Required actual observations, QMS results and traceability records exist.
- Exceptions and limitations are dispositioned sufficiently for the decision authority.

## Procedure
1. Freeze the Premium RainWater criteria/version.
2. Confirm the assurance scope covers the evidence required by the criteria.
3. Confirm the exact event, sample, batch and reporting population.
4. Review required field observations and instrument measurements.
5. Review admissible QMS/laboratory results.
6. Review sample custody, batch and seal traceability.
7. Review DMRV-derived metrics and their input lineage.
8. Review exceptions, limitations and any conditional requirements.
9. Apply each criterion explicitly.
10. Record the decision, rationale, evidence references and criteria version.
11. Record decision authority and timestamp.
12. Lock the decision or place the event on HOLD/REOPENED when requirements are not satisfied.

## Decision outcomes
- **PREMIUM_CONDITION_SATISFIED**
- **PREMIUM_CONDITION_NOT_SATISFIED**
- **CONDITIONAL_PENDING_SPECIFIED_REQUIREMENTS**
- **INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE**
- **HOLD**

## Evidence boundary
Premium-condition status is a decision against defined evidence and criteria. It must not be inferred from rainfall opportunity, forecast data, API availability, a DMRV dashboard, blockchain anchoring or software readiness.

A Premium-Condition decision is not automatically regulatory approval or product release.

## Fail-closed
Do not issue a Premium-Condition decision when required observations/results are missing, criteria are not versioned, assurance scope is insufficient, custody/traceability is broken, or material exceptions remain unresolved.

## Current declaration
No S13.13 Premium-Condition decision is claimed. No quality result or premium status is fabricated.
