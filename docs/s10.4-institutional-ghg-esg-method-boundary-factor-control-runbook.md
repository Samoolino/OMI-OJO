# S10.4 — Institutional GHG/ESG Method, Boundary and Factor Control

## Purpose
S10.4 controls the accounting and disclosure methodology layer used by reconciled metrics. It prevents a GHG/ESG number from being presented without an explicit framework, boundary, method, factor provenance and effective version.

## Framework mapping
### GHG Protocol
Use as the accounting framework where applicable. The Corporate Standard establishes corporate GHG inventory requirements; organizational and operational boundaries determine what is included, with Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3 treated according to their defined accounting boundaries.

### IFRS S2
Use as a climate-disclosure mapping layer where applicable. IFRS S2 covers governance, strategy, risk management, and metrics and targets for climate-related risks and opportunities.

### ESRS E1
Use only where the applicable jurisdiction and reporting requirement require it, with the adopted/effective version recorded. ESRS mapping is not itself assurance or certification.

## Method record
Each method records methodology_id, framework, requirement reference, version/effective date, applicability, boundary, activity-data source, emission-factor ID/source/geography/period, calculation method, assumptions, uncertainty and review status.

## Boundary discipline
Supported boundaries include organizational, operational, site, project, value chain and administrative reporting grid. An administrative grid is a reporting construct and must not be substituted for an organizational or physical boundary.

## Factor governance
Emission factors require source, geography, period and applicability. Factors may be proposed, applicable, approved, superseded or blocked. A newer factor does not silently rewrite historical calculations.

## Change control
Method or factor changes require impact assessment, effective date/version, affected metrics and periods, successor calculations where material, and controlled disclosure/restatement handling.

## Fail closed
Block disclosure when framework/version, organizational or operational boundary, factor provenance, applicability, calculation reproducibility or material uncertainty is missing.

## Current standards context
GHG Protocol currently reports an ongoing GHG Protocol/ISO effort to develop a consolidated corporate standard; this is treated as a future controlled-version change, not as permission to overwrite current historical methodology. IFRS S2 remains a climate disclosure framework, not a measurement instrument.

## Physical boundary
This gate governs accounting/reporting methodology. It does not create environmental observations, water-quality results, harvest evidence, premium status, regulatory approval or product release.
