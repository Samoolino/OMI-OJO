# S14.4 — Continuous Assurance and Release-Status Review

## Purpose
Perform recurring, evidence-bounded assurance over an authorized release and determine whether its current status remains supportable.

## Sequence
1. Open the defined review window.
2. Freeze the authorized release scope and population under review.
3. Reconcile evidence lineage.
4. Review QMS, laboratory, custody, batch and seal status.
5. Review source provenance and methodology/boundary/factor versions.
6. Review regulatory authorizations and conditions.
7. Reconcile product, label, public and investor claims.
8. Review open exceptions and CAPA effectiveness.
9. Assess assurance materiality.
10. Record a scoped assurance conclusion.
11. Initiate release-status action if the evidence requires it.
12. Lock the review record.

## Conclusions
- ASSURANCE_CURRENT_WITHIN_SCOPE
- ASSURANCE_CURRENT_WITH_OBSERVATIONS
- CONDITIONAL_PENDING_REMEDIATION
- RELEASE_SCOPE_REDUCTION_REQUIRED
- RELEASE_SUSPENSION_RECOMMENDED
- INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE
- HOLD

## Fail-closed
A conclusion cannot exceed the reviewed evidence. Unresolved material QMS, traceability, provenance, methodology, regulatory, claim, CAPA or security issues require controlled review, conditional status or HOLD.

## Evidence boundary
Assurance is a scoped review conclusion. It is not a new environmental observation, certification, regulatory approval or product-quality determination beyond the evidence and authority reviewed.

Historical conclusions remain immutable in the audit trail. A later change creates a new review, reopening or successor status rather than rewriting history.
