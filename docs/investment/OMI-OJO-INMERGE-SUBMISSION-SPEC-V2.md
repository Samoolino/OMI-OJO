# OMI-OJO — INMerge Submission Specification v2

Submission-control document for the 2026 INMerge startup application. It maps the repository's institutional business logic to the requested application fields and protects against unsupported investment, ESG and carbon claims.

## Approved application description

> OMI-OJO is a climate-water intelligence and digital MRV platform that turns distributed water-resilience activity into traceable, institutionally reviewable evidence. It connects environmental data, technical measurements, physical water assets, ESG/GHG reporting and integrity anchoring. Lagos is the reference deployment; the platform is designed for Europe, the Caucasus and Central Asia. Premium adds methodology-ready environmental/carbon evidence.

## Claim controls

### Permitted

- climate-water intelligence platform;
- digital MRV/evidence infrastructure;
- technical measurement and reporting architecture;
- Lagos reference deployment;
- controlled validation programme;
- carbon-relevant activity data;
- methodology-ready evidence as a Premium capability;
- future environmental-credit support subject to eligibility and verification.

### Not permitted without new evidence

- certified/verified carbon credits;
- issued or retired carbon units;
- guaranteed carbon volume;
- guaranteed water production volume;
- verified environmental impact;
- historical commercial revenue not recorded;
- live data feeds that are only configured interfaces;
- potable/human-consumption water claims without treatment/QMS/regulatory evidence.

## Evidence-state vocabulary

Every material number or impact statement must be labelled as one of:

`CONCEPT | OBSERVED | FORECAST | MODELLED | CALCULATED | VALIDATED | VERIFIED | ISSUED | RETIRED`

The label must reflect the actual evidence state at submission time.

## Premium positioning

Premium should be presented as:

> **An evidence amplification layer for customers requiring deeper measurement continuity, QMS/water-quality records, methodology readiness, independent-review coordination and climate-finance documentation.**

Premium is not presented as a carbon-credit generator.

## Funding language

If INMerge's application requests total funding raised, use historical external capital only. A proposed US$50,000 validation programme is **not** raised capital.

If the grant is referenced, describe it as the programme's stated grant prize and do not represent it as awarded until an award is formally received.

## Deck QA gate

Before PDF submission:

1. verify all URLs;
2. verify founder/team information;
3. remove internal secrets and credentials;
4. verify all external statistics and citations;
5. distinguish proposed budget from historical funding;
6. distinguish concept carbon pathway from issued credits;
7. test product URL and demo;
8. export 16:9 PDF;
9. review on mobile and laptop;
10. archive the final PDF and source version in the VDR.

## External standard note

The carbon pathway is intentionally methodology-dependent. Current VCS materials require an applicable methodology, project description, baseline/additionality and monitoring framework, followed by validation/verification and the applicable issuance process. OMI-OJO therefore supplies evidence infrastructure rather than asserting automatic credit creation.
