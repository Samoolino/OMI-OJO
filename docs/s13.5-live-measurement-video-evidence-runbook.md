# S13.5 — Live Measurement & Video Evidence

## Purpose
Capture actual field measurements and event-linked video for the authorized collection event while preserving device, method, timestamp and provenance.

This gate does not create laboratory results, certify water quality, determine premium condition, or release product.

## Preconditions
- S13.4 collection is complete for the same event.
- Event and physical site identity are resolved.
- Authorized operator is present.
- Measurement device identity is known.
- Required video path is available.

## Procedure
1. Confirm S13.4 collection completion.
2. Confirm the measurement device identity and status/calibration reference where required.
3. Capture the actual field measurement; record value, unit, timestamp and method reference.
4. Do not substitute forecast, model, synthetic or copied values for the actual measurement.
5. Start and stop event-linked video according to the approved evidence procedure.
6. Link video identity and timestamps to the same event and physical site.
7. Reconcile measurement timestamp, video timing, operator record and device identity.
8. Lock the field evidence record after review.
9. Place the event on HOLD when provenance or evidence linkage is materially incomplete.

## Evidence boundary
A field instrument measurement is an instrument measurement, not a laboratory result. Video establishes recorded evidence of an event only when its provenance and linkage are controlled. Neither establishes premium condition or product release by itself.

## Fail-closed
Do not advance to sample identification/custody if the measurement or units are missing, device provenance is unresolved, required video linkage is unavailable, event/site identity is broken, timestamps cannot be reconciled, or a material exception remains unresolved.

## Current declaration
No S13.5 live measurement or video evidence is claimed by this repository implementation. No measurement value, video event, sample result, or environmental performance value is fabricated.
