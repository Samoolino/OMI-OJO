# OMI-OJO — Institutional Business Logic & Investment Evidence Framework

**Version:** 1.0  
**Milestone:** M-1 — Investor-Ready Evidence Core  
**Reference deployment:** Lagos, Nigeria  
**Expansion:** Europe + Caucasus + Central Asia  
**Commercial principle:** Evidence infrastructure first; Premium environmental-market evidence second.

## 1. Executive business logic

OMI-OJO converts distributed climate-water activity into institutionally usable evidence.

The commercial chain is:

`SOURCE → MEASURE → QC → RECONCILE → VALIDATE → REVIEW → VERIFY → ANCHOR → REPORT → ACCEPT → SUBSCRIBE / SCALE`

The company therefore sells four connected value layers:

1. **Data & Intelligence** — environmental data, forecasting, modelling and climate-water analytics.
2. **Measurement & Infrastructure** — physical nodes, telemetry, water measurements and QMS interfaces.
3. **Verification & Reporting** — deterministic evidence packages, DMRV, ESG/GHG reporting, VDR and institutional review workflows.
4. **Premium Environmental-Market Evidence** — enhanced evidence continuity and methodology-ready carbon-relevant data for eligible projects.

The first three layers are the base business. Premium is an expansion layer and is never treated as automatic carbon issuance.

## 2. The product is the evidence spine

Customers do not buy a dashboard alone. They buy a traceable system connecting:

`ACTIVITY → TECHNICAL MEASUREMENT → EVIDENCE → INSTITUTIONAL DECISION`

Every material evidence object should contain:

- evidence_id;
- project/site/node identifiers;
- timestamp and geographic reference;
- variable, value and unit;
- evidence class;
- source/provider/instrument;
- methodology/version;
- QC and reconciliation state;
- uncertainty/assumption reference;
- parent/derived evidence references;
- package hash;
- reviewer state;
- release state.

Evidence classes are explicit:

`OBSERVED | FORECAST | MODELLED | SATELLITE | CALCULATED | PROXY`

## 3. Measurement-to-business conversion

### Layer A — Reporting
A customer needs a defensible report.

**Output:** public-safe, ESG/GHG, investor/VDR, regulatory or operational report.

### Layer B — Technical measurement
A customer needs the number to be reproducible.

**Output:** source, instrument/method, timestamp, location, unit, calibration/QMS and reconciliation record.

### Layer C — Verification infrastructure
A customer needs the evidence independently reviewable.

**Output:** deterministic evidence package, reviewer workflow, status machine and integrity proof.

### Layer D — Product proof
A customer needs proof that the physical/software product actually performed.

**Output:** controlled test, field dataset, QMS evidence, acceptance record and release decision.

### Layer E — Environmental-market amplification
A customer needs deeper evidence for an eligible environmental-credit/climate-finance pathway.

**Output:** methodology-ready evidence index, baseline/additionality records, monitoring inputs, calculation lineage and verification coordination.

## 4. Proof ladder

`P0 CONCEPT → P1 TECHNICAL → P2 FIELD/PRODUCT → P3 INSTITUTIONAL`

## 7. Premium Carbon Evidence Amplification

Premium is explicitly an **evidence amplification product**.

It may strengthen:

`MEASUREMENT FREQUENCY → PROVENANCE → QMS → BASELINE → ADDITIONALITY → MONITORING → CALCULATION → REVIEW`

It does not skip:

`METHODOLOGY ELIGIBILITY → VALIDATION → VERIFICATION → ISSUANCE → RETIREMENT`

Approved claim:

> OMI-OJO provides carbon-relevant activity data and methodology-ready dMRV infrastructure that can support eligible environmental-credit projects subject to applicable methodology, validation, verification and registry requirements.

Not approved without independent evidence:

> OMI-OJO generates certified carbon credits.

## 8. Carbon logic for water projects

Water capture, storage or reuse is not itself a carbon credit.

A carbon pathway must establish, where applicable:

- project boundary;
- baseline/counterfactual;
- additionality;
- eligible activity;
- emission factors and sources;
- monitoring variables;
- uncertainty;
- leakage/reversal treatment;
- double-counting controls;
- methodology/version;
- validation/verification;
- registry eligibility and issuance.

OMI-OJO therefore treats carbon as a downstream methodology layer over measured water activity.

## 12. Institutional status contract

`DESIGNED → IMPLEMENTED → INTEGRATION_READY → CONTROLLED_TEST → REPORTABLE → VALIDATED → VERIFIED → ANCHORED → RELEASED`

`HOLD` and `DEFERRED` prevent unsupported promotion.

A configured integration is not a live feed. A forecast is not a measurement. A blockchain transaction is not environmental verification.
