# S8.1 — Site Onboarding and Local Authorization Runbook

## Objective
Admit proposed sites to the controlled multisite program only when their identity, authorization, accountability and applicable technical/regulatory pathways are explicit.

## Admission procedure
1. Create a unique proposal and site ID.
2. Classify the node as administrative, environmental reporting, or physical field site.
3. Record geography and verify physical coordinates when a physical site is proposed.
4. Record the applicable local authorization and its validity/scope.
5. Identify the accountable operator and collector where collection is planned.
6. Confirm collector readiness before physical collection.
7. Identify local regulatory/product-release requirements.
8. Identify the QMS/laboratory pathway where water/product testing applies.
9. Register environmental data providers and source boundaries.
10. Assign a unique DMRV profile and controlled anchor configuration.
11. Record the admission decision, authority and timestamp.
12. Generate and preserve the integrity digest.

## Admission states

**SITE_READY** means the defined admission controls are satisfied for the stated scope. It does not mean a rain event occurred, water was collected, a batch was produced, or product was released.

**HOLD/REJECTED/DEFERRED** prevents physical operation until the recorded deficiency is resolved.

## Geographic boundary
A municipality, district, landmark or model/reporting grid can be represented as an administrative/reporting node without claiming that a physical collector exists there.

## Fail-closed rule
No physical collection proceeds from an unverified site identity, missing authorization, undefined accountability, missing applicable regulatory scope, or incomplete QMS pathway.
