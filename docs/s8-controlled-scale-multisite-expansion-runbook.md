# S8 — Controlled Scale and Multisite Expansion Runbook

## Objective
Extend the OMI-OJO / Blue-Ether operating model beyond the reference Lagos proof pilot through controlled, site-specific onboarding while preserving evidence integrity.

## Scope
S8 covers expansion architecture, site onboarding, local authorization, operational readiness, data-source reconciliation, QMS pathways, DMRV identity and evidence anchoring. It does not create physical harvest evidence by configuration alone.

## Replication rule
The Lagos reference pilot provides **control semantics**, schemas and operating procedures. It does not provide environmental measurements, water-quality results, harvest volumes, coordinates, regulatory approvals or other evidence values for another geography.

## Site classification
Every expansion record must distinguish:
- administrative node;
- environmental reporting node;
- physical field site;
- actual harvest;
- batch/seal;
- DMRV package;
- blockchain evidence anchor;
- release/verification status.

An administrative municipality or geographic grid is not automatically a physical installation.

## Procedure
1. Define the expansion scope and target geography.
2. Create unique site identities.
3. Verify geospatial identity where a physical site is proposed.
4. obtain/record local authorization before physical collection.
5. Confirm operator and collector readiness.
6. Establish local environmental data/provider mappings.
7. Establish the local QMS/laboratory pathway.
8. Configure site-specific DMRV identity and evidence package boundaries.
9. Configure controlled blockchain anchoring.
10. Run a controlled pilot before multisite operation.
11. Sample expanded operations through independent assurance.
12. Promote only the scope supported by evidence and authorization.

## Fail-closed principle
A site remains outside physical operation when authorization, identity, readiness, QMS, regulatory pathway or evidence controls are incomplete.

## Geographic policy
International project surfaces may describe planned administrative/reporting coverage, but they must not imply that every mapped node is a physical harvest site or that evidence exists where it has not been collected.
