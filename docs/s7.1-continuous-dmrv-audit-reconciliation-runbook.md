# S7.1 — Continuous DMRV and Audit Reconciliation Runbook

## Objective
Maintain a continuous reconciliation layer between released-batch records, DMRV evidence, blockchain anchors, QMS/product records, environmental observations, public claims and audit findings.

## Preconditions
1. S7 monitoring controls exist.
2. Release and batch/seal identities are known for any batch represented as released.
3. DMRV package and successor chain are available.
4. Anchor references are available where required.
5. New monitoring observations carry source, timestamp, evidence class and quality metadata.

## Procedure
1. Open a reconciliation period and create a unique reconciliation ID.
2. Reconcile release, batch, seal, DMRV and anchor identities.
3. Compare new observations against prior evidence without rewriting historical records.
4. Verify evidence classes and provenance for every material indicator.
5. Reconcile QMS/product records and audit findings.
6. Check public claims against the exact authorized release scope and evidence.
7. Surface material conflicts, gaps and anomalies.
8. Open an exception where required.
9. For a material correction, create a successor DMRV package and successor anchor referencing the prior version.
10. Record the reconciliation decision and integrity digest.

## Evidence handling
OBSERVED, FORECAST, MODELLED, SATELLITE, CALCULATED and PROXY evidence retain their original classification. Material source conflicts must remain visible and receive a documented disposition.

## Fail-closed controls
Stop reconciliation closure when provenance is missing, identities mismatch, the anchor chain is broken, evidence classes are inconsistent, material conflicts remain unresolved, public claims exceed evidence scope, audit findings lack disposition or a material correction lacks a successor package.

## Claim boundary
Continuous reconciliation improves auditability; it does not itself establish product safety, regulatory compliance or environmental benefit.
