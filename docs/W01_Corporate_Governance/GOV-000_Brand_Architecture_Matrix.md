# GOV-000 — Brand Architecture Matrix

**State:** S2 — SPECIFIED

## Purpose

Define the Blue-Ether brand architecture as a climate-tech operating system connecting distributed atmospheric/water harvesting, telemetry, quality management, DMRV and ecological outcomes.

## Architecture

- **Blue-Ether OS:** parent operating system and governance layer.
- **Blue-Ether Water:** harvesting hardware, cartridges and water operations.
- **Blue-Ether Data:** telemetry, forecasting, analytics and DaaS.
- **Blue-Ether DMRV:** environmental evidence and audit interfaces.
- **Blue-Ether Regeneration:** ecological and social outcome measurement.

## Governance rule

Commercial claims, ESG claims and public-facing performance claims must reference an approved source document and evidence state. No marketing artifact may upgrade an S2 assumption to an S4 verified fact.
