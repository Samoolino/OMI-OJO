# S9.5 — Governed Scale Assurance Execution and Disclosure Control

## Objective
Controlled execution of review evidence and climate/ESG disclosure preparation.

## Sequence
REVIEW SCOPE → EVIDENCE LOCK → LINEAGE RECONCILIATION → GHG CONTEXT CHECK → DMRV/QMS REVIEW → CONTROL EFFECTIVENESS → EXCEPTION/CAPA → INDEPENDENT REVIEW → DISCLOSURE MAPPING → CLAIM AUTHORIZATION → PUBLIC/INVESTOR RECONCILIATION → PERIOD CLOSE.

## Data contract
Every quantitative metric retains entity, reporting period, boundary, value, unit, evidence class, source, methodology/version, uncertainty or estimation context, evidence references and review state.

## Climate / ESG
IFRS S2 mapping covers governance, strategy, risk management, metrics and targets. GHG accounting preserves organizational and operational boundaries, Scope 1/2/3, activity data, emission factors, calculation method and change history. ESRS E1 records the applicable adopted version, reporting perimeter and effective period.

## Claim control
Claims require defined scope, supporting evidence references, locked review population, methodology/version, limitations and an in-scope review conclusion.

## Change control
Material changes create linked successor records. Historical records are never silently overwritten.

## Fail closed
Block promotion when evidence scope changes after review, lineage breaks, material exceptions remain unresolved, required GHG context is missing, methodology changes lack controlled transition, review conclusions are absent/out of scope, or public/investor surfaces differ from the authorized record.

## Physical boundary
Physical/product status remains NOT_RELEASED.
