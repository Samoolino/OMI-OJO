# S7.4 — Governance and Periodic Assurance Review Runbook

## Objective
Review the complete operating-control system at defined intervals so that changes in evidence quality, regulation, authority, risk, competence and system integrity are formally assessed before continued operation.

## Preconditions
1. S7.1 reconciliation, S7.2 CAPA and S7.3 assurance records are available for the review period.
2. Current policy and criteria revisions are identifiable.
3. The authority matrix and risk register are current enough for review.
4. Material regulatory or governance changes can be assessed.

## Procedure
1. Create a unique governance-review ID and freeze the review period.
2. Record scope, policy revisions and decision authority.
3. Review release records, DMRV integrity, anchor continuity, monitoring, exceptions, CAPA and independent audits.
4. Review regulatory/authority changes relevant to the operating scope.
5. Assess material risks, resource capacity and competency.
6. Identify conditions required for continued operation.
7. Record explicit risk acceptance only where authorized.
8. Place affected systems or releases on HOLD when material unresolved issues prevent continuation.
9. Record the next review date and accountable authority.
10. Preserve the governance decision and integrity digest.

## Decision semantics

### CONTINUED_OPERATION
The reviewed scope may continue under the recorded controls.

### CONDITIONAL_CONTINUATION
Operation may continue only subject to explicitly recorded conditions and owners.

### HOLD
Operation or the affected release scope is suspended pending disposition.

### RETIRED
A component or scope is retired with historical evidence preserved.

## Fail-closed controls
Unassessed material regulatory change, ignored material exceptions, unapproved criteria revisions, undocumented risk acceptance, unresolved DMRV integrity issues or material competency gaps prevent unconditional continuation.

## Claim boundary
Governance review is a control decision for a defined scope and period. It does not constitute universal regulatory compliance, product safety certification or proof of environmental performance.
