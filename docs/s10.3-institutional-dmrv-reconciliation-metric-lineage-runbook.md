# S10.3 — Institutional DMRV Reconciliation and Metric Lineage

## Purpose
S10.3 converts the locked reporting population into reconciled, traceable metrics. Every reportable metric must remain connected to its period, boundary, source, evidence class, methodology, calculation and review status.

## Metric contract
Required fields: metric_id, metric_name, reporting_entity, reporting_period, boundary, value, unit, data_type, evidence_class, source_id, methodology_id/version, calculation reference, uncertainty/estimation, evidence references, population_id, review status and lineage digest.

## Reconciliation sequence
POPULATION LOCK → METRIC REGISTER FREEZE → SOURCE RECONCILIATION → BOUNDARY/PERIOD RECONCILIATION → METHOD VERSION CHECK → CALCULATION REPRODUCTION → UNCERTAINTY REVIEW → EVIDENCE REFERENCE CHECK → LINEAGE DIGEST → OWNER REVIEW → INDEPENDENT REVIEW HANDOFF.

## Evidence-class discipline
OBSERVATION, FORECAST, MODEL_OUTPUT, SATELLITE, CALCULATED, PROXY and DISCLOSURE_DERIVED remain distinct. A model or forecast cannot be promoted to an observation through presentation-layer wording.

## Calculation lineage
Calculated metrics retain their input references, formula/method identifier and method version. Recalculation must be reproducible against the locked evidence population or a controlled successor population.

## Fail closed
Block reconciliation where unit, source, period, boundary or method context is missing; calculations cannot be reproduced; material uncertainty is omitted; lineage digest mismatches; evidence class is incorrect; or material exceptions remain unresolved.

## Claim boundary
A reconciled metric establishes traceability and review status. It does not by itself establish environmental truth, regulatory approval, premium water quality or product release.

## Physical boundary
S10.3 is a reporting-control implementation. No empirical rain, harvest, laboratory or batch result is created by this gate.
