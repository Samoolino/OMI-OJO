# S13.10 — Evidence Reconciliation

## Purpose
Reconcile the locked S13.9 DMRV event package before any blockchain anchor candidate is constructed.

The reconciliation evaluates whether the evidence items agree on identity, chronology, provenance, lineage and completeness within the defined event scope.

## Procedure
1. Confirm that the S13.9 package is locked and its scope is frozen.
2. Verify physical site identity and event identity across all records.
3. Reconcile source observations and their provenance.
4. Reconcile field measurements with instrument identity and timestamps.
5. Reconcile event-linked video chronology where required.
6. Reconcile sample identity and custody transfers.
7. Reconcile QMS/laboratory admission and result records where available.
8. Reconcile batch, container and seal traceability.
9. Reconcile every DMRV-derived metric to its input evidence, method, boundary and factor version.
10. Classify exceptions and limitations by materiality.
11. Record the reconciliation conclusion and authority.
12. Lock the reconciliation record.

## Outcomes
- **SUFFICIENT_WITHIN_SCOPE** — required evidence reconciles within the defined scope.
- **SUFFICIENT_WITH_OBSERVATIONS** — reconciliation is sufficient but documented observations/limitations remain.
- **CONDITIONAL_PENDING_CORRECTION** — package requires specified correction before anchor candidacy.
- **INSUFFICIENT** — evidence cannot support the required scope.
- **HOLD** — unresolved material issue prevents progression.

## Evidence boundary
Reconciliation does not create or upgrade an environmental observation. It does not constitute external assurance, certification, premium-condition determination or product release.

A successful reconciliation means only that the reviewed evidence was reconciled to the stated conclusion within its defined scope.

## Fail-closed
Do not advance when identity, provenance, chronology, custody/QMS lineage, batch/seal linkage, derived-metric lineage or material exceptions remain unresolved.
