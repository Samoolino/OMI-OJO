# S8.2 — Site Operational Readiness and Controlled Pilot Runbook

## Objective
Establish that an admitted physical site can execute a defined controlled pilot under the same evidence and fail-closed semantics established for the Lagos reference pilot.

## Readiness sequence
1. Confirm S8.1 admission and authorization scope.
2. Confirm accountable operator and collector.
3. Verify site/equipment readiness.
4. Confirm rain-event watch and source provenance.
5. Confirm first-flush control.
6. Confirm controlled collection and sampling/custody procedures.
7. Confirm QMS/laboratory handoff.
8. Confirm DMRV and evidence-anchor identities.
9. Confirm incident, hold and rollback procedures.
10. Record readiness decision and authority.

## Controlled pilot
A pilot may begin only after readiness is recorded for the defined scope. The pilot sequence is:

**INSPECTION → RAIN EVENT WATCH → FIRST FLUSH → CONTROLLED COLLECTION → CUSTODY → QMS HANDOFF → DMRV PACKAGE → REVIEW → CLOSEOUT**

Each event remains site-specific. No measurement is inferred from a readiness record.

## Evidence boundaries
- Forecast/rain-event watch remains FORECAST until an observation is independently recorded.
- Collection readiness does not establish collection quantity.
- QMS readiness does not establish laboratory results.
- DMRV readiness does not establish verification.
- Blockchain readiness does not establish environmental truth.
- Pilot completion does not establish premium status or product release.

## Hold conditions
Stop or hold the pilot for authorization mismatch, unsafe/failed equipment, unresolved site identity, failed first-flush control, custody break, QMS failure, material source conflict, or any condition requiring regulatory/product review.

## Frontend engagement
The Production Control surface must expose S8.2 as an operational checklist and controlled-pilot sequence. UI state is descriptive and must not create empirical evidence.
