# S14.5 — Release-Status Change Authorization and Public-Surface Reconciliation

## Purpose
Control changes to the status or scope of an authorized release and ensure every outward-facing surface reflects the same authorized successor record.

## Sequence
1. Record the status-change trigger.
2. Freeze the current status and scope.
3. Reconcile assurance findings.
4. Review regulatory and QMS impacts.
5. Confirm the authority permitted to change status.
6. Record the new status decision.
7. Create a versioned successor record.
8. Reconcile public, product and investor surfaces.
9. Withdraw or supersede stale surfaces where required.
10. Lock the effective time.
11. Lock the complete audit trail.

## Status options
- AUTHORIZED_ACTIVE
- CONDITIONAL
- SCOPE_REDUCED
- SUSPENDED
- WITHDRAWN
- SUPERSEDED
- HOLD

## Fail-closed
No status change may be represented without trigger evidence, frozen scope, authorized decision authority, resolved regulatory/QMS impact, a successor record, synchronized outward surfaces and an effective time.

Historical records are preserved. Withdrawal does not delete the historical record.

## Evidence boundary
A release-status change is a governance/control decision. It does not create a new environmental observation and does not itself constitute regulatory approval, certification or product-quality evidence.

## Current declaration
No release-status change is claimed. No public or investor release claim is generated by this implementation.
