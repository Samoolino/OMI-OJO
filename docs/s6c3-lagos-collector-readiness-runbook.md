# S6C.3 — Lagos Collector Readiness Runbook

## Objective

Establish the controlled evidence required before a proposed rainwater collector can participate in a validation event.

This stage does **not** authorize harvesting and does not establish potability, Premium RainWater status, regulatory approval, or environmental-credit eligibility.

## Sequence

1. Confirm accepted S6C.1 authorization evidence.
2. Confirm accepted S6C.2 survey/GIS verification.
3. Register the collector and configuration version.
4. Inspect capture surface, drainage path and collection materials.
5. Inspect and record first-flush configuration.
6. Register storage/transfer containers and applicable material evidence.
7. Register instruments, calibration/verification records and telemetry path.
8. Complete operator, public-safety and weather-stop review.
9. Bind event/site/collector/custody identifiers.
10. Record PASS, HOLD or REJECTED with reviewer identity and evidence references.

## Required evidence

- collector asset identity;
- configuration/version record;
- inspection record;
- first-flush inspection;
- storage/container identity;
- applicable materials evidence;
- instrument identity and calibration/verification evidence;
- safety assessment;
- event and custody identifiers.

## Fail-closed conditions

Any unresolved authorization, location, safety, collector identity, first-flush, custody or material instrumentation issue places the gate on HOLD or REJECTED.

## Transition

`PASS -> S6C.4 CONTROLLED RAIN EVENT READINESS`

## Evidence boundary

A readiness PASS is evidence that the assembly passed this readiness review. It is not evidence that a rain event occurred, that water was collected, that the water is safe to drink, or that a Premium RainWater product exists.
