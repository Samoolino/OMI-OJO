# S7.2 — Exception and Corrective Action Control Runbook

## Objective
Provide a controlled response to material discrepancies, incidents and evidence failures detected through S7 monitoring and S7.1 reconciliation.

## Preconditions
1. A monitoring or reconciliation process can generate exception records.
2. The affected batch/release scope can be identified.
3. Evidence preservation and containment authorities are defined.

## Procedure
1. Create a unique exception ID and record the detection source.
2. Identify affected batches, releases, claims and evidence.
3. Assess severity and materiality.
4. Apply containment or HOLD where warranted.
5. Preserve the original evidence; never overwrite it.
6. Assign an investigation owner and record the investigation method.
7. Determine root cause only when supported by evidence.
8. Create corrective and preventive actions with accountable owners and due dates.
9. Define effectiveness criteria before closure.
10. Record effectiveness evidence and independent review where required.
11. Close only under authorized closure authority.
12. If evidence changes materially, create successor DMRV and anchor records and link them to the original exception.

## CAPA states
- **EXCEPTION_READY:** exception captured and awaiting disposition.
- **CONTAINED:** affected scope controlled.
- **INVESTIGATION:** evidence-based investigation underway.
- **CAPA_OPEN:** corrective/preventive actions assigned.
- **EFFECTIVENESS_REVIEW:** actions completed and effectiveness being tested.
- **CLOSED:** authorized closure supported by evidence.
- **HOLD / FAILED:** release or process progression is blocked.

## Fail-closed controls
Do not close a material exception without defined effectiveness criteria and evidence. Do not change historical evidence to resolve an exception. Do not leave affected scope unidentified.

## Claim boundary
CAPA closure demonstrates that a defined issue was addressed under the recorded process. It does not prove that future risk is zero, that regulatory approval exists, or that a product is safe solely because the exception was closed.
