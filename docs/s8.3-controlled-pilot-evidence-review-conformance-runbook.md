# S8.3 — Controlled Pilot Evidence Review and Conformance Runbook

## Objective
Determine whether an authorized controlled pilot produced a coherent, traceable and conforming evidence package suitable for the next controlled-scale gate.

## Review chain
**ADMISSION → READINESS → PILOT EVENT → OBSERVATION → FIRST FLUSH → COLLECTION → CUSTODY/QMS → DMRV → ANCHOR (IF USED) → CONFORMANCE REVIEW**

## Review procedure
1. Freeze the pilot review scope and identity.
2. Reconcile the S8.1 admission and S8.2 readiness records.
3. Reconcile event, observation, first-flush and collection records.
4. Reconcile sample identity and custody through QMS/laboratory evidence.
5. Reconcile batch/seal records where applicable.
6. Recompute/inspect the DMRV package and evidence digest.
7. Review source provenance and material conflicts.
8. Check evidence classes: OBSERVED, FORECAST, MODELLED, SATELLITE, CALCULATED, PROXY.
9. Review anchor integrity where an anchor exists.
10. Review deviations, incidents and corrective actions.
11. Review the public/investor claim boundary.
12. Record an authorized conformance decision.

## Decision semantics

**CONFORMING_PENDING_NEXT_GATE** means the reviewed pilot package meets the defined conformance controls for its scope. It does not mean the water is premium, potable, commercially released or regulatorily approved.

**NONCONFORMING/HOLD** prevents promotion until the stated deficiency is resolved through the applicable successor-evidence or corrective-action pathway.

## Frontend engagement

The Production Control surface presents S8.3 as an evidence-review matrix. It must show evidence class and provenance rather than converting review status into environmental truth.
