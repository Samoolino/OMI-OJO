# S10 — Institutional Disclosure and Assurance Operations

## Objective
S10 establishes the recurring operating layer after S9.6: reporting periods, ownership, assurance coordination, disclosure authorization, publication integrity and post-publication control.

## Operating cycle
REPORTING CALENDAR LOCK → ENTITY/BOUNDARY CONFIRMATION → PERIOD DATA CUT → DMRV RECONCILIATION → GHG METHOD/SCOPE CHECK → QMS/CUSTODY/EXCEPTION REVIEW → ASSURANCE COORDINATION → MANAGEMENT RESPONSE → DISCLOSURE PACK ASSEMBLY → AUTHORIZATION/PUBLICATION → POST-PUBLICATION MONITORING → PERIOD CLOSE/LESSONS.

## Operating roles
Disclosure Owner; DMRV Owner; GHG Data Owner; QMS/Custody Owner; Assurance Coordinator; Independent Reviewer; Legal/Regulatory Reviewer; Publication Authority.

Roles are separated where independence or approval authority requires it. Owner attestation never substitutes for independent assurance.

## Period state machine
PLANNED → OPEN → DATA_CUT → UNDER_RECONCILIATION → ASSURANCE_REVIEW → DISCLOSURE_REVIEW → AUTHORIZED → PUBLISHED → CLOSED.

A closed period can only move to REOPENED through controlled change/restatement governance and a successor record.

## Control domains
Reporting calendar, entity/boundary register, metric lineage, DMRV reconciliation, GHG accounting, QMS/custody, exceptions/CAPA, assurance scope, disclosure claims, change/restatement, publication integrity and access/audit trail.

## Fail closed
Do not authorize publication when period/boundary is undefined, material reconciliation is unresolved, methodology/version is missing, material exceptions lack disposition, assurance scope is undefined, a claim exceeds reviewed evidence, the publication differs from the authorized pack, or the approval/audit trail is incomplete.

## Physical boundary
S10 is an institutional reporting operation. It does not imply physical harvesting, bottling, laboratory results, premium status, regulatory approval or product release. Physical/product status remains NOT_RELEASED.
