# S13.14 — Regulatory / Product Release Gate

## Purpose
Provide the final controlled boundary between the S13 evidenced field event and any regulatory or product-release authorization.

This gate does not grant regulatory approval merely because software controls are complete.

## Procedure
1. Freeze the release scope, jurisdiction and product/batch population.
2. Identify the applicable regulatory/product requirements and required authorizations.
3. Verify that required authorizations are current and cover the exact release scope.
4. Reconcile product specification, labeling and claims to the actual product/batch record.
5. Verify QMS/laboratory evidence required for the release decision.
6. Verify complete batch, container and seal traceability.
7. Verify the S13.13 Premium-Condition decision where the product claim depends on it.
8. Reconcile public and investor-facing claims to the authorized release record.
9. Review and disposition all material exceptions.
10. Record the release decision, authority, scope, limitations and timestamp.
11. Authorize, condition, reject or HOLD the release.
12. Lock the release record. Any later material change requires reopening or supersession.

## Outcomes
- **RELEASE_AUTHORIZED**
- **CONDITIONAL_PENDING_REQUIREMENTS**
- **RELEASE_NOT_AUTHORIZED**
- **INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE**
- **HOLD**

## Evidence boundary
Regulatory/product release is jurisdiction- and scope-specific. Software readiness is not regulatory approval. A Premium-Condition decision does not itself release a product. Blockchain anchoring does not create regulatory authority.

The release gate also does not create environmental observations.

## Fail-closed
No release authorization is represented when jurisdictional requirements, required authorizations, product/label consistency, QMS/laboratory evidence, traceability, premium-condition basis, claims reconciliation, material exceptions or decision authority are unresolved.
