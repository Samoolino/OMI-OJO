## Slide 1 — OMI-OJO
### Climate-Water Intelligence, Measurement & Digital MRV Infrastructure

**Built from Lagos. Designed for EuroAsia. Structured for global deployment.**

OMI-OJO turns distributed water-resilience activity into traceable evidence for ESG/GHG reporting, institutional decision-making and eligible environmental-market pathways.

**Core chain:** Data → Measurement → Verification → Reporting → Institutional acceptance.

## Slide 2 — The institutional problem

Climate-water projects generate sensors, field activity, forecasts, calculations and reports, but these evidence layers are frequently disconnected.

Institutions need to answer:

- What happened?
- Where and when did it happen?
- How was it measured?
- What methodology was used?
- Was the result reconciled and reviewed?
- What can safely be reported or financed?

**OMI-OJO supplies the evidence spine between physical activity and institutional trust.**

## Slide 3 — The solution

OMI-OJO connects:

`ENVIRONMENTAL DATA → TECHNICAL MEASUREMENT → PHYSICAL WATER ASSETS → QC/RECONCILIATION → DMRV → ESG/GHG REPORTING → INTEGRITY`

The platform complements sensors, laboratories, regulators and independent verifiers; it does not replace them.

## Slide 4 — Business logic

`SOURCE/SITE → OBSERVE → MEASURE → QC → RECONCILE → VALIDATE → REVIEW → VERIFY → ANCHOR → REPORT → ACCEPT → SUBSCRIBE/SCALE`

**The product is the evidence infrastructure, not a dashboard alone.**

## Slide 6 — Technical measurement

Every material evidence object carries, as applicable:

`evidence_id | project/site/node | timestamp | geography | variable | value | unit | evidence class | source/instrument | methodology/version | QC | reconciliation | uncertainty/assumption | parent evidence | package hash | reviewer state | release state`

Evidence classes:

`OBSERVED | FORECAST | MODELLED | SATELLITE | CALCULATED | PROXY`

A forecast never becomes a measurement merely because it is displayed in a report.
