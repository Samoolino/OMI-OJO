# S13.6 — Sample Identification & Chain of Custody

## Purpose
Create a uniquely identified sample record from the actual controlled collection event and preserve custody continuity until formal QMS/laboratory handoff.

This gate does not create a laboratory result or establish premium condition.

## Preconditions
- S13.5 field evidence is locked for the same event.
- Event and physical site identity are resolved.
- An actual sample exists from the controlled collection.
- Authorized personnel can establish initial custody.

## Procedure
1. Confirm the locked S13.5 field record.
2. Create a unique sample identifier; never reuse an identifier.
3. Link the sample to the event, physical site and collection record.
4. Record container identity and seal/integrity reference where applicable.
5. Record the initial custodian and sample creation timestamp.
6. Record every transfer with sender, receiver, timestamp and location/context.
7. Obtain receiving-party acceptance.
8. Reconcile the complete custody chain.
9. Lock the custody record before QMS/laboratory handoff.
10. Place the sample/event on HOLD if any material custody gap or integrity exception remains unresolved.

## Evidence boundary
Sample identification and custody establish traceability and continuity. They do not establish chemical, microbiological, physical or other laboratory properties. A custody record is not a laboratory result and is not a premium-condition determination.

## Fail-closed
Do not advance to QMS/laboratory handoff when the sample identifier, event linkage, container identity, custodian, transfer timing, receiving acceptance or custody continuity is unresolved.

## Current declaration
No S13.6 sample is claimed by this implementation. No sample identifier, custody event, laboratory result or environmental value is fabricated.
