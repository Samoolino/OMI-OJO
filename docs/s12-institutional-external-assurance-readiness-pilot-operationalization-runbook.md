# S12 — Institutional External Assurance Readiness and Pilot Operationalization

## Purpose
Prepare the institutional DMRV and Lagos reference pilot for controlled external assurance access and real field operations.

## Sequence
ASSURANCE SCOPE → EVIDENCE ROOM → CONTROL MATRIX → ACCESS/ROLES → SOURCE/METHOD REGISTER → SITE AUTHORIZATION → OPERATOR READINESS → LIVE CONNECTOR VALIDATION → RAIN EVENT/HARVEST → VIDEO EVIDENCE → QMS/CUSTODY → DMRV PACKAGE → ANCHOR CANDIDATE → EXTERNAL REVIEW → FINDINGS/CAPA → OPERATIONALIZATION.

## Evidence room
The controlled package must identify site, coordinates, rain event, weather and atmospheric sources, harvest measurement, first flush, video evidence, sample/custody/QMS records, batch/seal, DMRV package, ESG/GHG methodology, provenance and exceptions.

## Video evidence
Video is an evidence stream only when its capture time, source/device identity, event ID and integrity reference permit linkage to the controlled harvest/post-collection event. A video feed itself does not prove quantity, quality, compliance or environmental impact.

## Blockchain boundary
A deterministic DMRV package may produce an anchor candidate after package integrity checks. The anchor provides integrity and lineage; it does not establish environmental truth, water quality, certification, regulatory approval or funding.

## External assurance boundary
Reviewer access and assurance readiness are controlled separately from the review conclusion. Findings require disposition. No positive assurance conclusion is fabricated by the application.

## Pilot operationalization
Operationalization requires site authorization, operator readiness, trusted connectors, controlled rain-event procedure, measurement capture, video linkage, QMS/custody handoff, DMRV reconciliation and authorized disposition.

## Fail closed
Hold if scope/access is not authorized, site identity or provenance is unresolved, feeds are untrusted, rain/harvest evidence is absent, video cannot be linked, QMS/custody is incomplete, DMRV package is incomplete, material findings remain unresolved, or anchoring is attempted before package integrity.

## Non-equivalences
Readiness ≠ verification. Pilot readiness ≠ environmental performance. Forecast ≠ observation. Administrative node ≠ physical site. Anchor ≠ environmental truth. Operationalization ≠ regulatory approval/product release.
