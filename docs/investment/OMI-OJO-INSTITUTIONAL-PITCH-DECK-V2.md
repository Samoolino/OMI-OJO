## 02 — THE INSTITUTIONAL GAP

### Climate-water projects produce data. Institutions need evidence.

Today, environmental programmes often have separate:

- weather/environmental data;
- sensors and physical assets;
- field records;
- calculations;
- ESG/GHG reports;
- verification workflows;
- investor/funder evidence.

The missing layer is a **traceable evidence spine** connecting what happened in the field to what an institution is willing to accept.

**OMI-OJO is designed to be that evidence/control layer.**

---

## 03 — THE SOLUTION

### One controlled chain from activity to institutional report

```text
SOURCE / SITE
     ↓
OBSERVATION
     ↓
TECHNICAL MEASUREMENT
     ↓
QUALITY CONTROL
     ↓
RECONCILIATION
     ↓
VALIDATION / REVIEW
     ↓
DMRV EVIDENCE PACKAGE
     ↓
INTEGRITY ANCHOR
     ↓
```

OMI-OJO does not replace laboratories, regulators, sensors or independent verification bodies. It makes their evidence interoperable, traceable and reviewable.

## 12 — CONTROLLED CARBON PATHWAY

```text
CONCEPT
   ↓
ACTIVITY DATA
   ↓
METHODOLOGY ELIGIBILITY
   ↓
BASELINE + ADDITIONALITY
   ↓
MONITORING PLAN
   ↓
CALCULATED IMPACT
   ↓
VALIDATION
   ↓
MONITORING
   ↓
VERIFICATION
   ↓
ISSUANCE
   ↓
RETIREMENT
```

OMI-OJO can provide carbon-relevant activity data and evidence infrastructure. A water intervention becomes a carbon credit only if an applicable methodology and the relevant validation, verification and registry pathway support it. fileciteturn6file0L1-L2
