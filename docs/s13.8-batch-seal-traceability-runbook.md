# S13.8 — Batch & Seal Traceability

## Purpose
Create a unique batch record and controlled container/seal traceability after the QMS/laboratory handoff has been accepted.

This gate establishes identity and traceability. It does not certify quality or release product.

## Preconditions
- S13.7 QMS/laboratory handoff is accepted for the same event/sample.
- Event, physical site and sample identities are resolved.
- Approved batch and container/seal procedures are available.

## Procedure
1. Confirm accepted S13.7 handoff.
2. Create a unique batch identifier.
3. Link the batch to the event, physical site and sample.
4. Record actual quantity only when supported by an identified measurement record.
5. Assign container/unit identifiers.
6. Apply the approved seal/integrity mechanism where required and record its identifier and application time.
7. Reconcile event, sample, batch, container and seal identities.
8. Lock the traceability record.
9. Place the batch/event on HOLD when a material identity, quantity or seal exception remains unresolved.

## Evidence boundary
Batch identity is traceability evidence. Seal identity is integrity/traceability evidence. Neither is a laboratory result, certification, premium-condition determination or product release.

Actual quantity must retain its instrument/source provenance and cannot be invented to complete the batch record.

## Fail-closed
Do not advance to DMRV package assembly when QMS handoff is not accepted, identifiers are unresolved, quantity provenance is missing, seal application cannot be evidenced, or reconciliation is incomplete.

## Current declaration
No S13.8 live batch, quantity, seal or product unit is claimed by this implementation. No batch quantity or quality result is fabricated.
